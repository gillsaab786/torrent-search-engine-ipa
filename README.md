# Torrent Search Engine

Ad-free torrent search for iPhone, with every feature included.

- Searches 22 sources in parallel, results de-duplicated:
  - General: Knaben (meta-search over 1337x, The Pirate Bay, RARBG archive and more), ApiBay, BitSearch, Torrents-CSV, LimeTorrents, TorrentDownloads, Rutor, BT4G, TheRARBG, Internet Archive
  - Behind Cloudflare (tap **Verify** once in the app): 1337x, EXT, Torlock, TorrentFunk, KickassTorrents, UIndex, EZTV
  - Movies: YTS
  - Anime: Nyaa, SubsPlease, TokyoTosho, AnimeTosho
  - Optional: your own Jackett or Prowlarr server (hundreds of trackers)
- Categories, sort (seeders / size / newest / name), source filter, minimum seeders (default 3)
- Search history (swipe to delete), saved torrents, swipe between categories
- Open magnet in your torrent app, copy or share it
- Editable mirror URLs and tracker list
- Appearance: 13 accent colours, with a matching home-screen icon

## Install

**Download:** [TorrentSearchEngine.ipa (latest)](../../releases/latest/download/TorrentSearchEngine.ipa)

### Free, auto-refreshing (recommended): SideStore
1. Set up SideStore on your iPhone once (see sidestore.io; needs a PC for the first setup).
2. In SideStore → **Sources → +**, add:
   `https://github.com/gillsaab786/torrent-search-engine-ipa/releases/latest/download/source.json`
3. Install Torrent Search Engine from the source. SideStore re-signs it every 7 days on the phone, and offers updates when a new version is released.

### From a PC: Sideloadly
Download the IPA above, open Sideloadly, plug in your iPhone, drag the IPA in and sign in with your Apple ID.
With a free Apple ID the app must be re-installed every 7 days.

Search results come from public indexes. You are responsible for what you download.
